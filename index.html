SouthCross

A trip planner for journeys with more than one stop, for iPhone.

A trip is a sequence of things that happen: places you stay, moves between them, things you do, and ideas you have not scheduled yet. SouthCross keeps them on one map and one timeline, shows every time in the zone it happens in, and on the day tells you where to be and when to leave.

Your trips live on your device and in your own iCloud. There is no account, no analytics, and nothing is collected.
