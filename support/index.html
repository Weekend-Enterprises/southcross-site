Support

SouthCross is a trip planner for journeys with more than one stop.

Getting started

Tap + to create a trip with a name and dates. Open it and add places with the + button inside the trip: a stay, a move, something to do, or an idea for later. The map and the timeline follow each other.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything you have planned is readable without a connection. Maps and photos you have already looked at stay available.

Where are my trips stored?

On your device, and in your private iCloud if iCloud is on. There is no account to create.

Why does it ask for calendar access?

Only if you turn on "Show in Calendar" for a trip. The app creates and maintains a calendar of its own and touches nothing else.

The time looks wrong

Times are shown in the zone of the place they happen in. In Today mode, if your phone's clock and the itinerary disagree, SouthCross shows both and lets you choose.
