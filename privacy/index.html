Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

SouthCross is a trip planner for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your trips are yours, they stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and we never see them.

What we collect

Nothing. SouthCross has no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking software. We do not run servers that receive your data.

Where your data lives

Trips, notes, attachments and checklists are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they are also stored in your private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account; Apple's iCloud terms apply, and the developer cannot read it.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Calendar. Only if you turn on "Show in Calendar" for a trip. SouthCross then creates a calendar of its own and keeps it up to date. It reads and changes events in that calendar only.
  • Live Activities. Used to show a countdown on the lock screen while a trip is under way.

Network requests

To show maps, photos, weather and local context, the app talks to these services. None of the requests contain your name, an account or a device identifier.

  • Apple Maps and Apple Weather (through Apple's frameworks): place search, directions and forecasts for the coordinates of places in your trip.
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons: the name of a place, to fetch a photo of it. Photo credits are shown in the app.
  • Nager.Date: a country code, to list public holidays.
  • Frankfurter (European Central Bank rates): currency codes, to show an exchange rate.

Children

SouthCross is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
